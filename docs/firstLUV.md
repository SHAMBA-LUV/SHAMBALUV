## **Rule one: keep your private key private**

**Never share it.** Anyone with your private key controls your wallet and every LUV in it — today's LUV and
every LUV that ever arrives. There is no reset, no support ticket, no "forgot my key". The chain does not
know who you are; it only knows who can sign.

- **No one from SHAMBA LUV will ever ask for it.** Not by email, not by Telegram, not in a DM, not "to verify
  your airdrop". Anyone who asks is stealing.
- **Never paste it into a website.** Not ours, not a "checker", not a "claim portal", not a wallet-drainer
  dressed as a sweepstake. The only place your key belongs is inside a wallet *you* control.
- **Never photograph it to a cloud.** A screenshot in a photo library synced to three services is a key
  published three times.
- **Write it down, offline.** Paper or metal, two copies, two places — the same way people have kept
  seed phrases since 2009. Paper does not get phished.
- **Then treat the wallet as the sovereign thing it is.** Your signature is your identity — that is how
  [BANKON](https://bankon.pythai.net/) verifies who you are, and how LUV knows that a gesture came from *you*.
  Sovereignty over custody; consent over default. **Your folder belongs to you.**

If you lose the key, the LUV is gone the way the 4.98 quadrillion at `0x…dEaD` is gone — provably, forever,
to nobody. Please don't send yours there by accident.

## **Rule two (the better one): put your key in a trust for release at a later time**

Keeping a key private protects LUV from *other people*. Putting the key beyond your own reach for a stated
term protects LUV from the one person a private key cannot guard against — **you, on a bad day.**

A **trust** is any arrangement in which the key is held so that it **cannot be used until a date you chose
in advance**. It can be as formal or as simple as you like:

| form | how it works |
|---|---|
| **a sealed envelope** | Key on paper, sealed, dated, and given to someone you trust with written instructions: *do not open before ___.* The oldest trust there is. |
| **a safe-deposit box / lawyer's escrow** | Same envelope, held by an institution or an attorney under a release instruction. Formal, dated, witnessed. |
| **a legal trust or will** | The key (or its location) placed in an estate instrument with a release condition — a date, an age, an event. This is how LUV outlives you and reaches the people you meant it for. |
| **split custody** | Split the key into shares (Shamir's secret sharing) held by several trustees, each with the same release date. No single person — including you — can reconstruct it early. |
| **the LUVlocker (on-chain)** | The extend-only vault: lock the principal for a term, keep earning reflections the whole time. Use it when the [FAQ](https://luv.pythai.net/faq.html) links a *live* lock — earmarked is not locked, and a lock is a transaction anyone can verify. |

Choose the date honestly. A year is a real commitment. Five years is a statement. The point is not the
instrument — it is that **the release date is chosen now, calmly, and cannot be moved by the person you will
be on the day the price does something.**

## **Why this matters — three things a locked key does**

### **1. It protects LUV**
A key that cannot be used cannot be phished, drained, panic-sold, or talked out of you. Every attack on a
wallet is an attack on *access*; a trust removes access for the term. Your LUV sits at your address, on the
verified contract, [visible to anyone](https://luv.pythai.net/faq.html#contracts) — and touchable by no one.

### **2. It shows your commitment to LUV**
Holding is visible. Your balance is a public line in the holders table; a balance that does not move
through every dip is the on-chain shape of trust. In the language of the whitepaper's
[TRUST section](EMOTONOMICS.md#trust--the-extension-of-luv), a trust *is* the trust game's first move —
LUV extended forward in time before the return is known — and the earliest movers carry the most risk and
have the most to return. A key in trust says, in a way no tweet can: **I am here for the term, not the tick.**
Community actions are the measure of community sentiment; the most legible action of all is the one you
*don't* take.

### **3. It creates permanent value**
- **Every LUV not sold is depth for everyone.** LUV that cannot move is LUV that cannot be dumped; the pool
  under every other holder is deeper for it, and each exit is cushioned for those who remain.
- **The 3% pays you to hold.** Every market trade reflects 3% to holders — a locked balance earns it the whole
  term, with no action required. Time in the pool is the yield.
- **Metcalfe compounds it.** Value grows with the square of the connected participants. A holder who stays
  through a term is a node that stays in the network while *n* grows — the bridges you formed keep counting.
- **A term ends; the value doesn't.** When the trust releases, you hold LUV in a network that is larger, deeper,
  and older than the one you entered — and you have proven, on the ledger, that you were there from the start.
